# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all contributors and users, regardless of identity and background. We value respectful communication, thoughtful collaboration, and inclusivity.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and constructive in communication.
- Assume good intent and provide actionable feedback.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and learning objectives.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, or exclusionary conduct or language.
- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others’ private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of project collaboration.

## Consequences
Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent restriction from participation in this repository.

## Reporting
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the repository maintainers (the contributors listed in `README.md`). If you are uncomfortable reporting to the team, you may instead report to the course instructional team.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as possible.

## Enforcement
Maintainers will review reports and determine an appropriate response based on the circumstances.
